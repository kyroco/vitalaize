# A collector pointed at a board that does not take collectors: the setup
# finishes, the collector runs, and the first screen says why it is not
# paired and what to do on the hub.

wait 90 "What this Mac does"
click "Collector only"
click "Continue"
wait "Claude folders to include"
click "Continue"
wait "Hubs on your network"
type "Hub address" "{HUB}"
click "Continue"
wait "This Mac"
click "Start the collector and pair"
wait 120 "The collector is set up, but it did not pair."
expect "That board does not take collectors. On the hub, open its settings, turn on Take collectors, and ask again."
wait 30 "The collector is running"
wait 30 "Not paired with a hub yet, so nothing is sent."
snap c01-closed-hub
click "Pair with a hub…"
wait 5 "Pick the hub, or type its address."
disabled "Pair"
click "Close"
gone 5 "Pick the hub, or type its address."
