# Remove VitalAIze from a collector.

wait 40 "The collector is running"
press "Remove VitalAIze…"
wait 5 "Remove VitalAIze from this Mac?"
snap c13-remove-sheet
press "Remove"
wait 60 "VitalAIze was removed from this Mac"
expect "Stopping the collector"
snap c13-removed
click "Set up again"
wait 90 "What this Mac does"
