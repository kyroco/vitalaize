Kyroco VitalAIze

VitalAIze puts your builds and your Claude and Codex sessions on one screen, made for an iPad on the wall. It keeps every session's numbers, so you can see how your team's work trends over days and weeks.

What this installs

  • One app, VitalAIze, in your Applications folder.
  • On a Mac that never had VitalAIze, nothing runs yet, and nothing changes until you finish setup in the app.
  • On a Mac where VitalAIze is already set up, this replaces the app. Your settings and your database stay as they are.

What happens next

When the install finishes, VitalAIze opens by itself. If it is already set up, it restarts your board or collector on the new version and says what it did; there is nothing to answer. The first time, it asks what this Mac does:

  • Hub and collector, for your main Mac: it runs the board and saves this Mac's sessions.
  • Hub only: it runs the board and keeps what other Macs send it.
  • Collector only, for your other Macs: it sends this Mac's sessions to a hub.

It fills in what it can find on its own, like your Claude and Codex folders and your GitHub repository. Setup takes about a minute.
